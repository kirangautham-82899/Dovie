# Real-phone checklist

Run on the deployed site (offline mode only works in a production build), on at least one Android phone with
Chrome and, if you can, one iPhone with Safari. Use a test account. Note the date, phone and browser.

| Date | Phone and browser | Tester |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

## Install

- [ ] The site offers to install, or the browser menu has **Install app** / **Add to Home Screen**.
- [ ] The home-screen icon and name are right.
- [ ] It opens full screen, signed in, with nothing hidden under the notch or bottom bar.

## Offline

- [ ] Open Today, Planner, Money and Journal while online.
- [ ] Turn on aeroplane mode: a "You're offline" bar appears.
- [ ] Reopen the app: the pages you visited still open.
- [ ] A page you never visited shows the friendly offline page.
- [ ] Turn the connection back on: the bar goes and fresh data loads.

## GPS workout

- [ ] Start an outdoor walk. The location prompt appears and, once allowed, a route is recorded.
- [ ] Walk at least 500 m: distance and time look right to within about 10%.
- [ ] Lock the screen for two minutes, unlock: note what was and wasn't recorded.
- [ ] Pause, resume and finish. The session and route appear in history after a reload.
- [ ] Deny location on a second try: Dovie explains, and nothing breaks.

## Sign-out clears cached data

- [ ] Sign out from Settings.
- [ ] Turn on aeroplane mode and reopen the app: **none of the account's pages or data appear**, including via
      the back button.
- [ ] Back online, sign in as a second test account: nothing from the first account shows anywhere, including
      quick notes and savings coach choices (these are kept on the device).

## General

- [ ] Sign up, the confirmation email link and Google sign-in all return to the app.
- [ ] Forms aren't covered by the keyboard.
- [ ] Both themes are readable outdoors.
- [ ] The advice notes show on Fitness, Diet, Period tracker, Mental health and the Savings coach.

All boxes ticked = item 10 of the release-readiness report is done.
